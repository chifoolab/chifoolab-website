Mapping the system

The system overview of food education for children in Switzerland

A collaborative map of the stakeholders, programmes and initiatives that shape the food environment of children in Switzerland — at local, cantonal and national level.

What is this map for?

Making the invisible visible, building bridges

Bring into view

Shine a light on the national, cantonal and local stakeholders of food education.

Create synergies

Identify complementarities and opportunities for collaboration between existing initiatives.

Find your bearings

Get oriented within the landscape before launching new projects.

Communicate more effectively

Direct the right message to the stakeholders who are genuinely affected.
