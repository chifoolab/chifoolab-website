The Lab

A social laboratory for transforming food education

The Lab takes a systemic approach with committed stakeholders to jointly transform the food education system for children.

How can food education for children in Switzerland be made more holistic and effective?
Our approach

A systemic and collective approach

The Children's Food Education Lab's holistic approach aims to raise the awareness of stakeholders about the challenges of today's food education system for children, to make targeted use of synergies, and to foster systems thinking. At its core is the joint implementation of effective interventions and the strengthening of a collective understanding of the system. The Lab is also committed to exploring new funding opportunities and positioning the topic on the political agenda. Regular exchange between stakeholders is essential to driving this change forward.

Children

Childhood — a critical window

Childhood is a sensitive and especially suitable window for developing healthy and sustainable eating habits. These habits often shape eating behaviour well into adolescence and adulthood. Participatory involvement of children's perspectives in developing measures to strengthen food education increases their relevance and strengthens the target group's identification with these offers. That is why the Lab places great importance not only on involving the adults who create a health-promoting environment for children, but also on actively engaging children themselves in developing and shaping these measures.

Key stakeholders

A wide range of committed stakeholders

The Lab brings together people active at different levels of food education for children in Switzerland. The Lab is an open space that grows organically. Food education is a shared societal responsibility. No single stakeholder can drive this transformation alone.

Families

Parents and caregivers at the heart of everyday food skills learning

Canteens & chefs

Professionals who shape the food offered to children

School & teachers

Educators who bring food education into the classroom and the school garden

Doctors & health professionals

Paediatricians, dietitians and health professionals who support families and children

Local stakeholders

Producers, farmers and associations who make appreciation for food tangible

Industry & retail

Food producers and retailers who shape the range and availability of products available to children

Politics and institutions

Cantons, municipalities and decision-makers committed to the environment, health and education

Leisure activities

Camp supervisors, sports coaches and others who shape children's food environment

Research

Connecting scientific findings with lived realities
